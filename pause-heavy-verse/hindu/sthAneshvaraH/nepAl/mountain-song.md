Mountain Songs  
In the rush to live, don’t forget to live;  
Tired from the burdens of the day, don’t miss the dawn;  
Watch the golden rays descend from the treetops,  
Feel the whispering breeze,  
The first leaves of autumn brushing the ground,  
Softly making a soundless sound;  

In your blind race through life,  
Don’t miss the empty nests as the nestlings take flight;  
So many singers in the spring,  
So few left by autumn;  
Families remain; guests depart;  
Some songs are too familiar, too old;  
Some songs are new, and fleeting;  
The same breeze feels different  
When it caresses the bare skin—  
Some carry the warmth of remembered summers,  
Others the chill of approaching winter;  

Make some time for the lake,  
Turn over the stones as in the old days,  
Smile alone in the company of those  
Who left you,  
Yet whom you have kept, still adorned, within your memory.  

Let dusk cast its gold toward the east,  
Watch the light turn back toward its source;  
Celebrate every sunset as though it were the first;  
Don’t miss being here, now,  
In your endless rush  
To live in fantasy.
